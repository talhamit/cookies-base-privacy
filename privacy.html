# Cookies Base Scheduler — Privacy Policy **Last updated:** October 18, 2026 ## Overview Cookies Base Scheduler is a personal tool designed to help its owner organize Pinterest Pin drafts and scheduled publishing. ## Information We Handle The application may store Pin titles, descriptions, image file paths, destination URLs, scheduled times, and account labels entered by the owner. If Pinterest API access is enabled, the application may process account information and authorization tokens needed to provide the requested Pinterest integration. ## How Information Is Used Information is used only to manage the owner's drafts, schedules, and authorized Pinterest operations. We do not sell personal information or Pinterest data. ## Data Security The application is designed to keep its dashboard accessible only through the owner's local connection. Credentials and authorization tokens should be stored securely and must not be published in this repository. ## Data Retention and Deletion The owner may remove locally stored drafts and account information through database maintenance. Information may remain in backups until those backups are deleted. ## Third-Party Services If Pinterest API integration is enabled, Pinterest's own terms and privacy practices also apply. ## Contact For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the GitHub profile: https://github.com/talhamit --- This policy describes the intended use of Cookies Base Scheduler. It must be updated if the application's actual data collection, hosting, or sharing practices change.
